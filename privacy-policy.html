02

How We Store Your Data

CalTrack AI is built on an offline-first, zero-data-loss architecture:

On-Device Local Database — All food logs, daily summaries, custom foods, workouts, and progress records reside locally in a high-performance SQLite database (WatermelonDB). Your data is fully accessible offline.
Cloud Synchronization & Rolling Backup — When connected to the internet, your logs synchronize securely with Google Cloud Firestore under your authenticated user ID. This ensures seamless data restoration when updating the app or switching devices.
Media Storage — User avatars and progress physique photos are stored in private Firebase Storage buckets with authenticated access rules.
03

AI Features & Inference

CalTrack AI provides AI food recognition, barcode lookup, and natural language food logging. Here is how your data is handled:

Transmission — Food images and text descriptions you submit are transmitted over encrypted HTTPS to the Google Gemini API strictly to identify ingredients and calculate estimated calories and macronutrients.
No Model Training — Your private health data, food logs, body metrics, and progress photos are never used to train public machine learning models.
No Sale to Advertisers — Scanned food data and dietary habits are never sold, rented, or traded to data brokers or advertising networks.

Subscriptions, AutoPay & Billing

CalTrack AI offers optional premium subscriptions (CalTrack Pro) providing advanced features including instant AI camera scans, custom meal planners, in-depth nutrient breakdowns, cloud backup, and an ad-free experience.

Google Play In-App Billing & AutoPay — All subscription transactions, recurring payments, and Google Pay AutoPay authorizations are processed securely by Google Play. CalTrack AI does not handle, store, or process your credit card or bank details.
Subscription Plans & Auto-Renewal — Subscriptions are offered on monthly, annual, or promotional plans. Your subscription will automatically renew at the end of each billing cycle unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Google Play AutoPay will charge your chosen payment method automatically.
Free Trials & Promotional Codes — If you activate a free trial or redeem a promotional code (via Google Play Store promo code or developer grant), you will receive full Pro access for the specified duration. If you do not wish to be billed upon trial conclusion, cancel your subscription at least 24 hours prior to the trial end date in Google Play.
Cancellation Anytime — You can cancel your subscription at any time. Open the Google Play Store app > Tap your profile icon > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions > Select CalTrack AI > Tap Cancel subscription. Your Pro features remain active until the end of the current paid billing period.
Refund Policy — Because billing is managed directly by Google Play, all refund requests are subject to Google Play's standard refund policies. You can request a refund directly through the Google Play Store or Google Support.
Restoration of Purchases — If you upgrade your device, reinstall CalTrack, or change phones, tap Restore Purchases in the subscription or settings screen to restore your active entitlements immediately.
