Privacy Policy

1 Information We Collect

To provide you with the best health and fitness insights, we may collect the following data:

  • Account Data: Name, email address, and user identifiers.
  • Health Metrics: Height, weight, BMI, and customized fitness goals.
  • Activity Data: Step count, daily calorie intake, and food logs.
  • Device Data: Crash logs, analytics, and advertising identifiers.

2 How We Use AI (Important)

CalTrack AI utilizes artificial intelligence to provide personalized dietary and fitness insights. Here is how your data interacts with our AI:

  • Your food logs and fitness goals are processed to generate personalized recommendations.
  • No Public Training: Your personal health data is strictly used for your account and is never used to train public AI models.

3 How We Use Your Information

We use your data strictly to make the app better for you:

  • To provide accurate calorie tracking and fitness functionality.
  • To manage secure user authentication.
  • To analyze app performance and fix crashes.
  • To display relevant ads in the free version of the app.

4 Third-Party Services

We partner with trusted industry services to run CalTrack AI. These third parties may collect device and analytics information according to their own privacy policies:

  • Analytics & Stability: Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics.

  • Advertising: Google AdMob, Unity Ads.
We do not sell your personal health data to data brokers or third-party marketers.
